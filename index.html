4-Stage AI Workflow

1. Weak-Signal Fusion: 24h rain (IMD) + river level (KSDMA) + 7d drain complaints (K-SMART).
2. Explainable Risk Scorer: Strict point allocation (+2, +1, +1, +1) ➜ 0-1 Low, 2-3 Med, 4+ High.
3. Municipal Review Trigger: Complaints > 5 with 0 rain points prompts proactive desilting.
4. Bilingual Advisory Layer: Generates concise citizen alerts in English and Malayalam with helpline 1070.

Planned & Validated with IBM BOB

Plan Mode

IBM BOB analyzed scaling from 6 demo wards to 100+ production wards:

  • Temporal sync: Reconciled 3-hourly nowcasts with continuous complaint logs.
  • Spatial basin mapping: Replaced uniform city reservoir with ward-specific gauges.
  • Complaint normalization: Recommended per-capita density weighting.
  • Hallucination guardrails: Enforced deterministic templates for Malayalam.
